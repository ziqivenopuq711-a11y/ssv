Information

SSV Network is a fully decentralized, open-source ETH staking network, based on Secret Shared Validator (SSV) technology. It provides an open and simple infrastructure for splitting and distributing a validator key between non-trusting nodes, eliminating single points of failure and allowing for superior performance.

The SSV Bug Bounty Program is designed to reward security researchers and white-hat hackers who responsibly disclose critical vulnerabilities that could compromise the security and integrity of the SSV Network protocol. This program covers smart contracts deployed on Ethereum Mainnet, testnet infrastructure, and core protocol libraries.

Program Scope Notice: Rewards are denominated in SSV tokens on Ethereum. All amounts are quoted in USD equivalent. The program operates under Immunefi's standard terms and conditions for responsible disclosure.

Program Rules

All participants in the SSV Bug Bounty Program must adhere to the following rules. Failure to comply will result in disqualification and possible forfeiture of rewards.

1
Responsible Disclosure: Vulnerabilities must be reported exclusively through the Immunefi platform. Do not disclose publicly before a fix has been deployed and a coordinated disclosure timeline has been agreed upon.
2
No Exploitation: Researchers must not exploit the vulnerability beyond what is necessary to demonstrate its existence. Any exploitation of user funds, disruption of services, or data exfiltration will result in disqualification.
3
Proof of Concept: All reports must include a clear proof of concept (PoC) demonstrating the vulnerability. Reports without a PoC are less likely to be rewarded. Use testnets for PoC demonstrations whenever possible.
4
Original Research: Only original, previously undisclosed vulnerabilities are eligible. Duplicate reports will receive no reward. The first reporter to submit a valid, unique vulnerability will be rewarded.
5
KYC & Sanctions: Researchers may be required to complete KYC verification before receiving rewards. Participants from sanctioned jurisdictions are not eligible for rewards.
6
Severity Classification: Severity is determined by the SSV team in collaboration with Immunefi based on impact, exploitability, and CVSS score. The team's determination is final.
