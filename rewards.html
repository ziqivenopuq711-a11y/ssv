Reward Structure & Tiers

Full reward structure for the SSV Bug Bounty Program: tiered rewards for smart contract and node vulnerabilities, critical reward calculation methodology, and payout details.

Reward Structure Overview

SSV Network provides rewards in SSV tokens on Ethereum, denominated in USD. Reward amounts are determined by the severity of the vulnerability and its potential impact on the protocol and its users. The SSV team and Immunefi jointly triage and classify each submission.

All reward amounts quoted below are in USD equivalent, paid out in SSV tokens at the time of payment. The conversion rate is determined at the moment of payment using a spot price from a recognized exchange. Researchers are encouraged to contact the team if they prefer an alternative payout arrangement.

Smart Contract Rewards

Smart contract vulnerabilities represent the highest severity category in the SSV Bug Bounty Program due to the direct financial risk to protocol users.

Critical
$50,000 – $250,000
Direct loss of funds, irreversible protocol disruption, or forced mass validator slashing
High
Up to $25,000
Significant but limited fund loss, temporary disruption, or unauthorized governance actions
Medium

Minor fund loss or degraded protocol functionality affecting a subset of users
Low

Critical Reward Calculation

For Critical vulnerabilities affecting Mainnet contracts, the reward amount is calculated as a percentage of the at-risk funds:

10% of directly affected funds, capped at a maximum of $250,000 and a minimum floor of $50,000.

This means a vulnerability that puts $1,000,000 in SSV tokens at risk would yield a $100,000 reward. A vulnerability that puts $100,000 at risk would still receive the $50,000 minimum, ensuring researchers are properly incentivized even for moderate-scale vulnerabilities.

Reward Adjustment Factors

  • Quality and completeness of the PoC
  • Accuracy of the impact assessment
  • Inclusion of a recommended fix
  • Speed of disclosure relative to active exploitation risk
  • Researcher's KYC completion and sanctions compliance
